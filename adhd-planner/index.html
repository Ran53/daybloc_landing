A visual time blocking planner for ADHD

If visual structure works better for you than a long task list, DayBloc gives your day a timeline you can see. It is a general productivity app, not a medical or ADHD treatment tool.

Why visual time blocking can be useful

Some people prefer seeing time represented spatially rather than scanning a long list. DayBloc is built around that visual model. Whether it suits you depends on your own planning style.

A note about ADHD

DayBloc is not a medical device, diagnostic tool, or treatment for ADHD. This page describes a planning approach that some people with attention or executive-function challenges may choose to try; individual needs vary.
