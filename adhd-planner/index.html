How to use a time blocking app

Time blocking is a planning method where you give an activity a place on your calendar or daily timeline. Instead of keeping every task in one list, you decide when you intend to work on it.

  1. List what matters today. Start with the work, appointments and personal activities that need time.
  2. Give each activity a time slot. Add a block to your timeline and choose a realistic duration.
  3. Use colors to make categories visible. Separate work, personal tasks, breaks and routines at a glance.
  4. Leave room to adjust. Move or resize blocks when something takes longer than expected.
  5. Review the day. Compare your plan with what actually happened and use that insight when planning tomorrow.
Keep your schedule realistic. A time blocking planner is a guide, not a test. Leave transition time between activities and avoid filling every minute of the day.
