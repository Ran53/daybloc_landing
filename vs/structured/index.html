DayBloc vs Structured

Both apps use a visual timeline, but their product models differ. This page focuses on the documented positioning of each app so you can decide which planning workflow fits you.

Get DayBloc on Google Play Explore DayBloc

DayBloc

DayBloc centers the experience on a visual timeline of draggable time blocks, routines, streaks, heatmap patterns and planned-versus-actual review. Its landing page says no account is required.

Structured

Structured describes itself as an all-in-one day planner that brings tasks and events into a visual timeline. Its current documentation also describes an inbox, recurring tasks, calendar integration and multiple timeline views.

The key distinction

DayBloc presents the timeline as the primary planning surface for time blocks. Structured combines its timeline with a broader task-and-event planning system.

Planning model

DayBloc emphasizes blocks of time and the shape of the day. Structured emphasizes tasks and events placed into its daily timeline, with an inbox for items that do not yet have a time.

Platform and ecosystem

Structured currently documents multiple platforms and device/web options; DayBloc is a visual time blocking app focused on simple daily planning.
