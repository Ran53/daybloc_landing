DayBloc vs Tiimo

DayBloc and Tiimo both use visual planning, but they are positioned differently. Here is a concise comparison based on their current public product descriptions.

Get DayBloc on Google Play Explore DayBloc

DayBloc

DayBloc’s landing page centers on a visual time-block timeline, draggable and resizable blocks, color, routines, streaks, heatmap patterns and planned-versus-actual review.

Tiimo

Tiimo describes itself as a visual planner for ADHD, Autism and anyone who benefits from flexible structure. Its current product pages describe calendars, to-dos, routines, reminders, focus timers, AI planning, widgets and cross-device syncing.

The key distinction

DayBloc is deliberately centered on time blocks and a lightweight visual schedule. Tiimo presents a broader planning toolkit that combines schedules, tasks, routines, focus tools and AI features.

If you want a focused time-blocking workflow

DayBloc’s interface is built around placing blocks on a timeline and keeping the planning system visually simple.

If you want a broader planning toolkit

Tiimo’s current product description includes a wider set of planning and focus features, including AI planning, to-dos, timers, widgets and cross-device sync.
