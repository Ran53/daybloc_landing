What does offline planning mean here?

DayBloc is designed so you can use the core planning experience without creating an account. If you are looking for a simple planner that does not begin with a registration flow, this is the model.

A visual alternative to a to-do list

A to-do list tells you what needs doing. Time blocking also asks when it will happen. DayBloc puts that second question at the center of the interface.
