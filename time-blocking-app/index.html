Why use time blocking?

Time blocking assigns activities to specific periods of the day. Instead of keeping every task in one undifferentiated list, you give important work a place on the timeline. DayBloc is built around that visual approach.

Designed to stay simple

DayBloc does not require an account to start planning. The core experience is a visual timeline where you can create, move and resize blocks, then reuse routines and review your patterns.
